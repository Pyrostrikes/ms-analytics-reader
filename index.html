MS Analytics Reader

MS Analytics Reader is a private, read-only tool that the channel owner runs on their own computer. It reads view and audience statistics for the owner's own YouTube channels and Facebook Pages so the owner can review how their videos perform.

  • It only reads statistics. It never uploads, posts, edits or deletes anything.
  • It is used only by the owner of the channels and Pages. It is not offered to the public.
