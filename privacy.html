Privacy Policy: MS Analytics Reader

Last updated: 27 November 2026

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads statistics for the owner's own YouTube channels (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, read-only scopes) and the owner's own Facebook Pages (Pages list, Page engagement and Page insights, read-only).

How the data is used

The statistics are saved only on the owner's own computer and used only to review the performance of the owner's videos. The data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account (Security, Third-party apps) or in Facebook settings (Business Integrations), and can delete the local files at any time.

Contact

This app is used only by the owner of the channels. Questions can be sent through the contact options on the owner's YouTube channels.
